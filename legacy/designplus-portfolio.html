How Horizon Logistics went from invisible to unmistakable

Horizon Logistics Ltd — Nairobi, Kenya
The challenge

Horizon Logistics had been operating for 8 years with a logo designed on Microsoft Word. Despite being one of the most reliable logistics firms in Nairobi, clients consistently underestimated their scale and professionalism at first impression.

Our approach

We conducted a full brand audit and competitor analysis, then developed a new visual identity system — logo, typography, colour palette, and brand guide. This was applied across a redesigned website, a full stationery suite, and a fleet branding programme covering all 22 vehicles.

How Afrique Media tripled their organic traffic in 90 days

Afrique Media Group — Nairobi, Kenya
The challenge

Afrique Media had strong editorial content but almost no online visibility. Their website was buried on page 4 of Google for their most important keywords, and organic traffic had flatlined for 18 months.

Our approach

We started with a comprehensive technical SEO audit, fixing 47 crawl errors and improving site speed by 62%. We then developed a keyword strategy targeting 28 high-intent search terms relevant to the Kenyan media market and built a content calendar to support each one consistently over 12 weeks.

How Pearl Properties turned social media into their top lead source

Pearl Properties Kenya — Nairobi, Kenya
The challenge

Pearl Properties had active social media accounts but no strategy — sporadic posting, inconsistent visuals, and almost zero engagement from potential buyers. Their sales team was relying entirely on word-of-mouth referrals.

Our approach

We rebuilt their social presence from the ground up: a new content strategy focused on lifestyle and aspiration, professionally designed post templates consistent with their brand, a 3-posts-per-week publishing schedule, and targeted paid ads reaching potential buyers in specific Nairobi neighbourhoods. Community management was handled daily to respond to every enquiry and comment.

12.4K New followers in 6mo
35+ Monthly enquiries
