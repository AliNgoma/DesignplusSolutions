Our story

We started small.
We thought big.

"Great design shouldn't be a luxury. Every business — from a street-side startup to a corporate boardroom — deserves to look the part."

Designplus Solutions was born out of a simple frustration: too many Kenyan businesses were invisible — not because their products were poor, but because their brand didn't do them justice.

We started with a small studio in Nairobi, offering graphic design to local businesses who couldn't afford big agencies. Word spread quickly. Clients came back. They referred their friends, their partners, their suppliers. And before long, we weren't just a design studio — we were a full creative and digital agency trusted by some of the most ambitious businesses in East Africa.

Today, Designplus Solutions offers everything from brand identity and web development to printing, signage, SEO, and social media management. But our ethos hasn't changed: we treat every client's business as if it were our own. Their goals are our goals. Their deadlines are our deadlines. Their success is our success.

We've worked with startups launching their first brand, established corporates refreshing theirs, NGOs communicating vital missions, and individuals building their personal profiles. Every project — no matter the size — gets our full attention and our best work.

We don't just deliver files. We build brands that people remember, trust, and choose — again and again.

How we got here

Our journey

Every milestone brought new skills, new clients, and a deeper commitment to the businesses we serve.

2017

Designplus Solutions is founded

Started with a laptop, a design tool, and a clear mission: to give Nairobi's small businesses the same quality of creative work that the big corporates enjoyed. Our first three clients became long-term partners.

2018

Web design & development added

Demand from clients who wanted their brand online led us to build our web team. Within six months, we'd launched 15 websites and were fully booked three weeks in advance.

2019

Print & signage division launched

We brought print and large-format signage in-house, giving clients a seamless end-to-end brand experience — from the logo concept to the billboard installation.

2021

Digital services expanded: SEO & Social Media

Post-pandemic, clients needed more than a brand — they needed to be found online. We built our digital marketing capabilities and began managing social media and SEO for growing businesses.

2023

Custom IT & Digital Solutions division launched

As client needs grew more complex, we added tailored IT and digital solutions — custom software, integrations, and tech consulting — cementing our status as a true full-service agency.

Today

150+ projects. 80+ clients. Still growing.

Designplus Solutions is now one of Nairobi's most trusted creative and digital agencies — and we're just getting started. East Africa, watch this space.
