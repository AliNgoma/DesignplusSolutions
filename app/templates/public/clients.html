Our approach

How we build relationships, not just deliverables.

Our 95% client retention rate isn't an accident. It comes from treating every client as a long-term partner — not a one-off transaction. Here's what that looks like in practice.

"The thing I value most about Designplus is that they push back when they think we're wrong — and they're usually right. That kind of honesty is rare."

Michael Ng'ang'aManaging Director, Eastgate Properties
01

We learn your business before we touch your brand

Every engagement starts with a deep-dive conversation about your business — your goals, your audience, your competitors, your challenges. We don't design in a vacuum. The more we understand about what you're building, the better the work we create for it.

02

We communicate proactively, not reactively

You'll never be left wondering where your project is. We give you a clear timeline upfront and provide regular progress updates throughout. If something changes, you hear it from us first — never after the fact.

03

We give honest advice, even when it's uncomfortable

If we think a direction won't serve your brand well, we'll tell you — respectfully, with reasoning, and with an alternative. We're not a yes-agency. We're a trusted advisor, and that means sometimes steering you away from a bad idea, not just executing it.

04

We think about your next step, not just the current one

The best creative work is built for the long term. We design brand systems that can grow with you — scalable, adaptable, and consistent — so you don't have to start from scratch every time your business evolves.

05

We stay available long after the project ends

Many of our best client relationships are with businesses we first worked with years ago. We offer ongoing retainer support for exactly this reason — so you always have a trusted creative team a call away, without having to brief someone new every time.
